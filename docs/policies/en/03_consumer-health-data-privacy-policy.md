# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 6, 2026

This policy explains how SSOL Tarot House handles information that could say something about your physical or mental health. It is written for Washington's My Health My Data Act, and we apply it to every user. It adds to our Privacy Policy; where they differ, this policy applies to consumer health data.

## What counts as consumer health data here

Your tarot cards and readings are wellness content, not health information. Some things you share with us, or that we work out from what you write, could still be linked to your wellbeing:

| Category | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you write about your situation | Anything you choose to say about stress, sleep, mood, your body, your mental health, or your safety | You, before drawing cards (optional) |
| What we work out from it | The feelings and needs your text shows (for example, feeling anxious or needing rest), and signs that you may be in crisis or facing violence | Created by our system and OpenAI from your text |
| Remedies and practices | The self-care suggestions in your report | Created from your cards and, if you wrote, your text |
| Sowella AI chat messages | Anything you choose to say about your mood, stress, sleep, body or mental health | You, in the chat |

We do not ask for medical records, diagnoses, medications or precise location, and we do not use geofencing.

## Why we collect and use it

- To write your reading and full report
- To tailor your Sowella AI chat and reply to you
- To spot writing that suggests a crisis or violence, pause the reading, and show you support contacts

We collect consumer health data only with your consent (the second checkbox, before you submit what you write) or as needed to provide the service you ask for. We never use it for advertising, and we never send it to Google Analytics or X.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with third parties or affiliates for their own use. If that ever changes, we will ask for your separate consent first.

Only the service providers that run the Service for us process it, under contract and only on our instructions:

| Provider | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Supabase, Inc. | Database (Mumbai region, India) |
| Vercel Inc. | Website hosting |
| OpenAI, L.L.C. | Sorts what you write, writes parts of your reading, and writes Sowella AI replies |
| Supabase, Inc. and Vercel Inc. (India) | Database and hosting for Sowella AI |

## Your rights

You can:

- confirm whether we collect or share your consumer health data, and see it, including a list of every third party and affiliate it was shared with and how to contact them;
- withdraw your consent;
- ask us to delete it. We also tell our service providers to delete it. Copies in backups are deleted within 6 months.

Email contact@ssolwellness.com with the subject "Health data request". We may ask you to confirm your identity. We answer within 45 days. If we need up to 45 more days, we will tell you why. Requests are free up to twice a year, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

**Appeals.** If we turn down your request, email us with the subject "Appeal" within 45 days. We will reply in writing within 45 days and explain our decision. If we deny your appeal, you can complain to the Washington State Attorney General at www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

## How we protect it

Only staff who need it can access consumer health data. We encrypt it in transit and at rest and protect it with access controls.
