# Refund Policy

**Effective date:** November 6, 2026

The full report (US$1.99) is a one-time digital purchase that unlocks right away, so it is usually not refundable once you have opened it. We do refund when something goes wrong on our side.

## When we refund in full

| Situation | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| You paid but the report did not unlock, and we cannot fix it within 3 days | Full refund |
| You were charged twice for the same order | The extra charge, in full |
| The report is badly different from what the product page described | Full refund, if you contact us within 3 months of purchase or 30 days after you notice |
| You have not opened the report yet | Full refund within 7 days of purchase |

## When we do not refund

Under our Terms of Use, when you buy the full report you ask us to deliver it right away and agree that you lose your right to cancel once it is shown to you. After the report is shown, we do not refund for reasons such as:

- you did not like the cards you drew or the reading did not match your expectations;
- you wanted a different direction than the one the cards pointed to;
- you wrote about a different situation than you meant, or wrote nothing (you can start a new reading at any time).

This follows Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Article 17(2). If you live in the EU or UK, you normally have 14 days to cancel a digital purchase; you give up that right only through the agreement above, once the report is shown.

## How to ask

Email contact@ssolwellness.com within the periods above with your order ID (in your receipt email), the email on your account, and what went wrong. We answer within 3 business days.

## How you get your money back

We refund to the original payment method, in the currency you paid. Card refunds go through Toss Payments; PayPal refunds go back to your PayPal account. Within 3 business days of approving a refund, we ask the payment company to cancel the charge. Your bank or PayPal may take a few more days to show it.

If we refund an order, the full report for that order is locked again.

## Card disputes

Please contact us before you dispute a charge with your bank. We can usually solve it faster. Your rights under the law and your card issuer's rules stay the same either way.
