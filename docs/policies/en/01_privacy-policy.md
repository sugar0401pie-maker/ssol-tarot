## At a glance

- **Writing about your situation is optional.** You can draw cards without writing anything. If you do write, you give separate, explicit consent first, and we use your words only for your reading and for your safety.
- **No birth details, no real name.** We ask only for a nickname. We never ask for your date of birth, address or ID number.
- **AI helps write your reading.** If you write about your situation, we send your text to OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States) to sort it and write parts of your reading. It is not used to train their models. If you don't write anything, no AI is used and your reading is put together from texts we wrote.
- **We check for signs of crisis.** If what you write suggests you may be in danger, we pause the reading and show you where to get help.
- **No account needed.** You can save results and pay without signing in.
- **We never sell your data.** We share it only with the services that run the site, take payments and measure our ads.
- **Your data may leave Korea.** Some of those services are in the United States, and Sowella AI runs on servers in India. The section "Where your data goes" lists them.
- **You stay in control.** You can see, fix or delete your data, and withdraw consent, at any time.

**Two consent boxes.** When you start, you see one unticked box confirming that you are 18 or older, agree to the Terms of Use and have read this Privacy Policy. If you choose to write about your situation, a second unticked box appears above the submit button. It is your separate, explicit consent: *"I agree that SSOL may use what I write, including anything about my health, relationships or safety, to create my reading and to check for signs that I may need help. My text is sent to OpenAI to help write my reading."* Next to it we show what we collect, why, and how long we keep it, with a link to the full Consent for What You Write. If you don't tick it, you can still draw cards without writing anything. You can withdraw it at any time; we then delete your text and what we worked out from it.

**Legitimate interests.** Where we rely on legitimate interests, those interests are keeping the Service secure and free of abuse, understanding which channels bring visitors, and answering your messages. We weigh them against your rights, and you can object at any time (see "Your rights").

**What you write is used for your reading only.** We do not use your text for ads. We use it to improve the Service only after removing anything that could identify you (see the Consent for What You Write).

**Please don't write other people's details.** Leave out full names, phone numbers, addresses and other details that would identify someone else. "My manager" or "my ex" is enough for your reading.

**Card details.** We never collect or store your card number, expiry date or CVC. Toss Payments and PayPal handle them on their own secure systems.

**What we do not collect.** We do not ask for your real name, date of birth, phone number, home address or ID number.

**How your reading is made.** Our server shuffles the cards at random. Your card readings, remedies and practices come from texts we wrote. If you wrote about your situation, an AI model from OpenAI sorts your text (for example, which area of life it's about) and writes the parts of your reading that connect the cards to your situation. Our code, not the AI, works out which way the cards lean. We check every AI-written part against our rules before you see it. If you don't write anything, no AI is used. Your reading is for self-reflection and does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

**Your share card.** If you share your cards, the share card shows only the card pictures, card names and positions. It never shows what you wrote, your feelings or your reading.

**Using your results in Sowella AI.** Sowella AI is run by SSOL Wellness House too, so this is use within our own services, not sharing with a third party. When you tap **Start a chat with Sowella AI** on your report, Sowella AI, which runs on servers in India, uses the items in the table above with the same account so it can greet you and tailor the chat. We do not pass any payment details. New Sowella AI members get a free 3-day pass under Sowella AI's own terms. Sowella AI writes its replies with models from OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States); we send OpenAI only these items and your chat messages, never your email or payment details. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train its models. Anything you share about your health is covered by our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. Sowella AI has its own terms and privacy notice. You can ask us to delete the tarot text and results Sowella AI uses by emailing contact@ssolwellness.com, or remove them with **Delete chat history** in Sowella AI.

## How we protect it

- **Management:** an internal privacy plan, as few staff with access as possible, regular training.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

- see the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- correct it if it is wrong;
- delete it, or ask us to stop or limit using it;
- withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what we did before.

You can view and edit your information in your account, and withdraw all consent by deleting your account. For anything else, email contact@ssolwellness.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We act without delay and reply within one month at the latest.

**EU and UK residents** also have the right to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

**California residents** also have the right to know what categories of data we collect, to opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising (our X ad measurement), to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. Use the **Do Not Sell or Share** link in the footer or turn off advertising cookies. We honor Global Privacy Control signals. We use what you write only to provide the reading you asked for.

**Korean residents** can also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (118, privacy.kisa.or.kr), the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972, www.kopico.go.kr), the Supreme Prosecutors' Office (1301, www.spo.go.kr) or the Korean National Police Agency (182, ecrm.cyber.go.kr).
