# Consent for What You Write

**Effective date:** November 6, 2026

We ask for this consent separately from the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, and only if you choose to write about your situation. What you write can include details about your health, your relationships or your safety, so we handle it with the same care as sensitive information.

You don't have to write anything. If you tap **I'd rather keep it in my head**, you still get a reading, and nothing below applies.

## 1. What we collect

- What you write about your situation (free text)
- What we work out from it: the area of life it's about, the feelings and needs it shows, the type of question, the options you mention, and whether it shows signs that you may be in crisis or facing violence

## 2. Why we use it

- To connect your cards to your situation and write your reading and full report
- To check for signs that you may be in danger, and if so, to pause the reading and show you where to get help
- If you start a chat, to tailor your Sowella AI conversation (Sowella AI uses your original text, your cards and your reading)
- To improve the Service with statistics, only in a form that cannot identify you (for example, how many readings were about work). **We never use your text for ads.**

## 3. Who processes it

Your text is sent to OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States), which sorts it and writes the parts of your reading that connect the cards to your situation. OpenAI acts only on our instructions, does not use your text to train its models, and deletes it after up to 30 days. It is also stored in our database (Supabase, Mumbai region in India). See "Where your data goes" in the Privacy Policy.

## 4. How long we keep it

Until you delete your account or withdraw this consent. Results saved without an account are deleted 30 days after saving (30 days after payment for paid results). Readings made without signing in that were never moved to an account are deleted 7 days after you made them. See "How long we keep it" in the Privacy Policy.

## 5. Your right to say no

You can refuse this consent. If you do, you can still draw cards and get a reading without writing anything. The reading will simply be more general.

## 6. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw at any time by deleting your account or emailing contact@ssolwellness.com. We then delete your text and what we worked out from it without delay. Records we must keep by law (such as payment records) don't include your text.

Anything not covered here follows the Privacy Policy.
