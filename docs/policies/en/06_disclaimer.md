# Disclaimer

**Effective date:** November 6, 2026

SSOL Tarot House is for entertainment and self-reflection. The cards are a mirror for your situation, not a prophecy. Please read your results as a prompt to think and act, not as facts about you or your future.

## Not professional advice

SSOL Wellness House offers non-clinical wellness coaching and wellness sessions. We don't provide psychotherapy, diagnosis or medical treatment.

Nothing on the Service is medical, psychological, legal, financial or investment advice. Do not use a reading to decide about your health, medication, money, investments, legal matters or your safety. For those, talk to a qualified professional.

## Tarot does not predict the future

Tarot is a symbolic tradition, not a scientifically proven way to predict events or describe personality. Your cards are drawn at random. A reading may talk about the next few months and point toward one of your options, but that is a way to think through your choice, not a forecast of what will happen or how other people will act.

## Where the cards point is a suggestion

SSOL Tarot House suggests actions. When your reading points toward one option, our code works it out from the cards you drew. It does not know the full facts of your situation, such as money amounts, contracts or what another person is thinking. The final decision, and what you do with it, is yours.

## How readings are made

Card readings, remedies and practices come from texts we wrote. If you write about your situation, AI helps connect your cards to it. Sowella AI replies are written by AI. Automated and AI content can be wrong, incomplete or not right for you. Use your own judgment.

## Your reading depends on what you write

The more clearly you write about your situation, the closer the reading fits it. If you don't write anything, or write very little, the reading is more general.

## If you are struggling

The Service is not for emergencies or crisis support. If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or feel unsafe, contact your local emergency number now. In the US or Canada, call or text 988. Elsewhere, find a helpline at findahelpline.com. If you need professional help with your mental health, please see a psychiatrist or another mental health professional.

## How we respond to signs of crisis

1. **We check what you write.** If you write about your situation, our server checks it with keyword rules and an AI review for signs of suicidal thoughts, self-harm or violence.
2. **We pause the reading and point you to real help.** When your text shows signs that you may be in danger, we stop the card reading, show crisis contacts for your country, and encourage you to see a mental health professional. SSOL's own wellness sessions are not offered as crisis support. When it shows signs of violence or threats, we show where to get help and never suggest reaching out to the person involved.
3. **We don't go into harmful detail.** Our readings and AI are instructed never to give information about methods of self-harm or suicide.
4. **We review and improve.** We review how these checks work and update them regularly with Korea-certified therapists and psychologists.

## How Sowella AI handles a crisis

Sowella AI is a wellness coaching chat for adults aged 18 and over. It may not be suitable for minors. A notice that you are talking to AI, not a human, stays on the chat screen the whole time. Every message, in English and Korean, is checked for signs of crisis; when it shows those signs, Sowella AI stops its usual coaching and shows crisis contacts for your country, such as 988 in the US and Canada.
